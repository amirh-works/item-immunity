# Item Immunity - Enchantment

A lightweight and essential datapack or mod that introduces the **Immutable** enchantment. Never lose your hard-earned endgame gear, shulker boxes, or rare items to accidental environmental hazards ever again!

---

## ✨ Features

The **Immutable** enchantment fully safeguards dropped items on the ground from being destroyed by the following vanilla hazards:

### 🔥 Lava & Fire Immunity
Items will float safely on lava or sit in fire without burning up.<br>
![Lava Protection](https://github.com/silverbreezed/item-immunity/blob/26.2/public/sword.GIF?raw=true)

### 💥 Explosion Immunity
Blocks and items dropped during creeper or TNT blasts remain completely intact.<br>
![Explosion Protection](https://github.com/silverbreezed/item-immunity/blob/26.2/public/explosion.GIF?raw=true)

### 🌵 Cactus Immunity
No more accidental item deletion when dropping items next to cacti.<br>
![Cactus Protection](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f1cd88f5946f4c3d2544d2df05d28cc937368045.png)

## Where to Find It?

The **Immutable** book is a rare and valuable treasure. You can obtain it through three primary methods:
* **Villager Trading**: Available as a rare trade from Librarian Villagers.
* **Ancient City**: Hidden deep within the deep dark structures as a reward for brave explorers.
* **Mineshaft**: Available on a minecart chests<br>

![Villager Trading](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/47c0aa662759b6834fb014b104182170189f3d40.png)

---

## 🛠️ Supported Items

You can apply the **Immutable** enchantment to almost any valuable item via an Anvil. Supported items include:

| Category | Equipment & Tools |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gear & Weapons** | All Armors, Weapons, Tools, Shields, Tridents, Mace, Bows, Crossbows, Fishing Rods, Shears, Carrot/Warped Fungus on a Stick |
| **Storage Utilities** | Shulker Boxes (All Colors), Bundles (All Colors), Ender Chests |
| **High-Value Items** | Totem of Undying, Golden Apples, Enchanted Golden Apples |

---

## ⚙️ Default Configuration
* **Max Level**: I (1)
* **Enchantment Weight**: 1 (Very Rare)
* **Exclusivity**: Conflicts with the *Vanishing Curse* (`minecraft:vanishing_curse`) by default.

## How to Install
<details>
<summary><b>Windows</b></summary>

**As a datapack**
1. Press `Win + R`, search `%appdata%\.minecraft`, then **Enter**.<br>
2. Go to the folder `saves` > `[Your World Name]` > `datapacks`.<br>
3. Place the `.zip` here.

**As a mod**
1. Press `Win + R`, search `%appdata%\.minecraft`, then **Enter**.<br>
2. Go to the folder `mods`.<br>
3. Place the `.jar` here.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>MacOS</b></summary>

**As a datapack**
1. Open **Finder**, press `Cmd + Shift + G`.
2. Put a path: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft`.
3. Go to the folder `saves` > `[Your World Name]` > `datapacks` and place the `.zip` here.

**As a mod**
1. Open **Finder**, press `Cmd + Shift + G`.
2. Put a path: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft`.
3. Go to the folder `mods` and place the `.jar` here.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Linux</b></summary>

**As a datapack**
1. Open `~/.minecraft/saves/[Your World Name]/datapacks`.<br>
2. Move the `.zip` to the folder.<br>

**As a mod**
1. Open `~/.minecraft/mods`.<br>
2. Move the `.jar` to the folder.<br>

</details>
